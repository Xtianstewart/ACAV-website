Privacy Policy

ACAV LLC — Phoenix, Arizona

Effective date: November 11, 2026

1. What this policy covers

This policy describes what information ACAV LLC ("ACAV," "we," "us") collects through phoenixavstaffing.com (the "Site"), our Crew Portal at crew.phoenixavstaffing.com (the "Portal"), and our ACAV Crew Ready text messaging program, and how we use it.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact and profile information you provide: name, phone number, email address, skills, tier, availability, and onboarding documents you upload (such as agreements and tax forms).
  • Inquiry information from clients and prospects who contact us about staffing services.
  • Messaging information: your mobile number, your text-messaging opt-in consent and its timestamp, and the messages exchanged with our Crew Ready line.
  • Website data: standard analytics and server data (pages visited, device/browser information, approximate location from IP address).

3. How we use it

We use this information to operate our staffing business: coordinating crew availability and assignments, responding to client inquiries, administering agreements and payments, sending the Crew Ready texts described in our Terms of Use, and improving the Site and Portal.

Text messaging data: We do not share, sell, or rent mobile phone numbers or text-messaging opt-in and consent data with third parties for their marketing purposes. Your mobile number is used only for the Crew Ready coordination messages you opted into, and by the messaging providers that deliver them on our behalf.

4. When we share information

  • With service providers who operate parts of our service (for example, our messaging provider Twilio and our hosting providers), only as needed to run the service.
  • With clients, limited crew information needed to staff a confirmed call (such as name, role, and contact number for the crew assigned to that call).
  • When required by law, or to protect our rights and the safety of others.

We do not sell personal information.

5. Your choices

  • Texts: reply STOP to opt out of Crew Ready messages at any time; reply HELP for help.
  • Access and correction: contact us to review or correct your information, or to ask that we delete your Portal profile.
  • Cookies: you can control cookies through your browser settings.

6. Data retention and security

We keep records as long as needed for our business and legal obligations (including tax and agreement records). We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the size of our business; no system is perfectly secure.

7. Children

Our services are for working adults in the event industry. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be posted at this URL with its effective date.
