ACAV Crew Ready — Text Message Opt-In

ACAV LLC — Phoenix, Arizona

ACAV Crew Ready is the text messaging program ACAV LLC uses to coordinate freelance AV crew availability. This public page shows the opt-in exactly as a freelancer sees it in the ACAV Crew Portal, so the consent step is visible without a login.

1. Step 1 — Check the SMS consent box on your Portal profile

When a freelancer creates a profile on the Crew Portal at crew.phoenixavstaffing.com, the profile form includes this SMS consent checkbox:

That checkbox, with that wording, is the consent a freelancer checks on their own profile before Crew Ready can be enabled.

2. Step 2 — Enable by keyword

After saving the profile with the consent box checked, the freelancer enables the service by texting a keyword to the Crew Ready number, +1 855-554-0444:

  • READY FULL — available for a full window (12 hours)
  • READY HALF — available for a half window (6 hours)

3. Program basics

  • What we send. Availability confirmations, job-match alerts, and crew-request notifications related to your profile and availability. This is a coordination service, not advertising.
  • Message frequency varies based on your availability status and matching work.
  • Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.
  • Opt out anytime: reply STOP to end messages. Reply HELP for help. You may also contact us at info@phoenixavstaffing.com or 480-330-0543.
  • Consent is not a condition of working with ACAV or of receiving our services; opting out only means we will coordinate with you by other means.
  • Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Full program terms are in our Terms of Use (§4, Text messaging — ACAV Crew Ready); how messaging information is handled is in our Privacy Policy.
