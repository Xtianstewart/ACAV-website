Staffing handled, not handed to you.

Planning an event already means a venue, a caterer, a run of show and a hundred decisions. Booking AV labor one freelancer at a time — chasing availability, rates and day-of confirmations person by person — should not be one of them. ACAV works as one staffing partner: you describe the event, we match the crew by role and confirm every position.

Crew confirmed within 24 hours, guaranteed. You get names and roles confirmed, rates locked in a written work order before event day, and one contact for changes. If the program grows, a room is added or the schedule shifts, you make one call and we re-work the crew plan — no day-before scramble, no unanswered group texts.

What is handled

The whole labor picture, covered.

Every role, one booking. Audio engineers, video and camera operators, lighting technicians, stagehands and working leads — matched to your program instead of pulled from a generic list.

The full event arc. Load-in, rehearsal, show and strike are covered in the same booking, with crew counts adjusted per phase: heavy hands for the build, operators for the show, an efficient crew for the strike.

Changes absorbed. Programs change. Added sessions, longer days, an extra room — tell your ACAV contact and the crew plan is reworked on our side, not rebuilt on yours.

A lead on site. Every event gets a working lead as your single point of contact on the day: crew checked in, briefed and placed before your first guest or speaker arrives.

How booking works

Four steps to a confirmed crew.

1. Send the event details. Date, venue and what the program needs — a run of show helps, a rough outline works. Use the form below or call 480-330-0543.

2. Get a quote & crew plan. We scope the roles, phases and crew size, and send a quote with rates in writing. No obligation until you approve it.

3. Crew confirmed. Once approved: crew confirmed within 24 hours, guaranteed — names, roles and call times locked before event day.

4. Event day. The crew arrives briefed and checked in by our lead. You run your event; we run the labor.
